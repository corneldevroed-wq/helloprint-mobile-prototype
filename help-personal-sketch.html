Signed-in help · concept A The rules

Your own orders, proofs and invoices in the article

Concept A: one block "Your …" right under the title. The action is often the real answer ("How do I approve my proof?" → this proof, approve it); the explanation below stays for whoever wants it. Same rows everywhere: number · product · status or deadline · one action, max 3 + "View all" underneath.

Use the switch on top: signed in · signed in with nothing open (the block disappears, except on status questions) · signed out (one quiet line). These are the real prototype pages; the block is only added in this sketch. Links go to #my-account-… (My Account doesn't exist yet).

The rules

  • One component for 11 kinds of data (proofs, changeable orders, order status, shipments, recently delivered, past orders, quotes, invoices, refunds, cases, account details); linked to the article by its slug. ~65 of 146 articles.
  • Under the title, above the text. Max 3 rows, the most urgent first; "View all" under the rows, to the matching page in My Account.
  • The block is light grey; every row is a white card with the grey card outline. One action per row: the light green button (.btn.primary) for the thing to do (Review proof, Pay now), the outline button for a quiet one (Download PDF, Edit).
  • Nothing relevant: no block. Only status questions (my complaint, my refund) say "nothing open" because that is the answer.
  • Urgency in words (deadline, days left), never a red or orange label: orange stays commercial.
  • Signed out: one grey line with a Sign in link, only on the ~25 "my" questions (proof, track, invoice, quote, complaint, refund).
  • Help Centre home, signed in: "Needs your attention" with the same rows, under the search (last example).
