Check 11 of the UI kit. Today styles.css uses 20 different corner values. Below: the proposed scale, every value now and where it would go, and the changes you would see.

Proposed scale: 6 values

4px · smallBadges, checkbox, flags, thumbnails and tiles inside a card
8px · controlFields, option cards, quantity rows, menus, small cards
10px · cardProduct, category, promo, content and summary cards, photos
16px · sheetThe sheet from the bottom (top corners), desktop panels
100px · pillButtons, pills, search, toggles, toast
50% · roundRadios, avatars, dots, round arrow buttons

0 stays where a photo is deliberately square (home and category product photos on desktop, Team + Promises images). As tokens: --r-s 4 · --r-m 8 · --r-l 10 · --r-xl 16 · --r-pill 100px.
