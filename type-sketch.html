My pick: this scale. The rule of thumb: 13 → 12 for notes and meta text (it is helper text, as the label size), 13 → 14 for links, buttons and rows (things you click); 15 and 17 → 16 for titles in cards on desktop and 14 on mobile (the card title rule); 18 → 20 for sheet and checkout titles (the mobile section title); 11 → 12. The 16px login field also stops iOS from zooming in. Build as the corners: tokens, every size on a token, a switch Type O/N, measured page by page. The dead banner CSS goes in the same round.
