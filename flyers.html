    Leaflet printing made better with creative folds

    Folded leaflet printing is an excellent way to present information in a structured and visually appealing manner. Perfect for businesses, events, or promotional campaigns, folded leaflets are versatile tools for sharing key details like product information, menus, or event schedules.

    With various folding options available, you can tailor your leaflets to fit your message. Choose a Half-fold for a simple brochure-style design, ideal for presenting a focused message. Opt for a Tri-fold to create six panels, offering plenty of space for organised content. The Z-fold adds a dynamic touch, making it easy to guide readers through step-by-step instructions or storytelling. For even more impact, consider a Roll-fold, which adds extra panels for detailed layouts.
