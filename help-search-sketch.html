Searching the articles on the topic pages

Three ways, each working with the real questions (topic: Ordering). Try "proof", "cancel", "invoice" or "xyz". Same field as the hub search (rounded, grey cross to clear), so it feels like one Help Centre.

A · Search in the sidebar, above the topics

Always in the same place on topic and article pages. Searches every article, suggestions drop down as on the hub. Calm and familiar, but it lives next to the content, not in it: on mobile the sidebar sits below the questions, so it would need to move to the top there.

B · Filter this topic, under the title my pick

The field sits where the reader already looks (under the title, where the mobile tabs were). Typing filters the questions on the page live, with the match highlighted; matches in other topics follow under "In other topics", so there is never a dead end. No matches anywhere: a friendly message + Chat with us.

C · A search band under the breadcrumbs

A light grey band over the full width ("Search all help articles"), like a mini version of the hub hero. Very clear, but it repeats the hub and pushes the questions down; on mobile it adds a block above the content.

My pick: B

  • People land on a topic with a question in mind; filtering the list they are looking at gives instant feedback without leaving the page.
  • "In other topics" catches the wrong-topic case (e.g. "invoice" on Ordering), the empty state hands over to chat: no dead ends.
  • Same position on mobile and desktop, reuses the hub field; no extra block, no dropdown covering the list.
  • Article pages: no field (the "Back to topic" link leads to it); the hub keeps its big search.
