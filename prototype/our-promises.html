We’re an online printing platform: we send your order to the right print partner, who prints it and delivers it to you. These aren’t empty words. Below we explain what each promise means, how it works and, to be honest with you, what we can’t guarantee.

Four promises at the heart of HelloPrint

For us it’s not just about printing. It’s about making every order a good experience for you: fair prices, good design and a team that cares.

sellCompetitive market prices

Seen a lower price? Let us know and we’ll look into matching it with our print partners. A fair, realistic price.

sentiment_satisfiedSatisfaction promise

Not completely happy? We’ll find a solution together: a free reprint, a full refund or 10% credit.

design_servicesAlways a perfect design

Good print starts with a good design. With our tools, anyone can create a professional design, even without any design experience. We also check your file for free and help with your design whenever you need it.

support_agentAlways here to help

We’re right by your side. Behind every order there’s a customer service team ready to help. They’re with you every step of the way and help you find the right print partner.

What we cannot guarantee

Our promises cover everything that depends on us and our print partners. A few things are out of our hands, and we’d rather be upfront about them:

descriptionThe file you send us

If your file has typos or layout mistakes, we can’t be held responsible. Always check it carefully: our file check helps, but the content is up to you.

edit_locationIncorrect delivery details

If the delivery address is wrong, we can’t guarantee your order arrives on time. Double-check your details before you confirm.

thunderstormExtreme weather

Storms or other extreme weather can delay couriers, and unfortunately that’s outside our control and our print partners’.

event_busyPeak seasons

At busy times, like Christmas or big campaigns, printing and delivery can take a little longer.
