School Fundraising Ideas for the Parents' Association

A wall calendar with every class photo sells to every family. A raffle runs on numbered tickets, and a cake sale needs only a poster at the gate.

Running a raffle? Sort this out before you print the tickets
  • A raffle at your event, with tickets sold and drawn on the night and no cash prizes, does not need a permit.
  • Bigger raffles and lotteries need a Garda permit or a District Court licence. With a permit, the prize value goes on every ticket.
  • Everyone taking part must be 18 or over.

General information, not legal advice. Check the current rules with Citizens Information and the Gambling Regulatory Authority of Ireland.
