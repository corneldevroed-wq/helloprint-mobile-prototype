Sustainability at HelloPrint

For us, sustainability isn’t a slogan. It’s a journey we measure, step by step. As a B Corp, we work with our print partners to reduce the impact of everything printed through our platform.

We believe great print and care for the planet can go hand in hand. That’s why we make it easy for you to choose more sustainable materials, send your order to a print partner close by whenever possible and share our progress openly.

Sustainability is part of who we are

We want every step of your order to be a little better for the planet: the materials you choose and the way it reaches you. Our print partners take care of printing; we make the sustainable choice the easy one for you.

hubWhat we do as a platform

We send your order to the right print partner and help you choose better: more sustainable materials, local production whenever possible and, for some products, an estimate of their CO₂ emissions.

local_shippingWhat our print partners do

They print, pack and deliver your order. Whenever possible, we send it to a partner close to you, so it arrives sooner and travels less. In Ireland, carriers such as An Post and DPD bring it to your door.

publicWhy it matters

Print has an impact, and we take responsibility for reducing it, in our own work and across our supply chain. That’s why we set concrete targets for the whole chain.

Officially a B Corp

We’re proud to be a B Corp, part of a global community of companies that use business as a force for good. For us it isn’t a label but a promise, checked against demanding social, environmental and governance standards.

We’ve been B Corp certified since 2024, so our sustainability ambitions are measured against an independent, internationally recognised framework.
