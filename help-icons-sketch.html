Question (Cornel): not a fan of the paper-style topic icons. Three directions on the same card (the material card) and the same mobile accordion as the Help Centre; only the icon changes.

My pick: A. Calm, clear and in the same family as the icons in the rest of the UI (search, chat, chevrons). It tells you the topic in one glance without becoming an illustration. B gives the "extra visuals" you're missing, but only works with real Weave mockups per topic: for Account and Complaints there is no product, so here they're placeholders that make the weakness visible. C is the calmest, but six text cards in a row read heavy, and the icon helps scanning on mobile.
