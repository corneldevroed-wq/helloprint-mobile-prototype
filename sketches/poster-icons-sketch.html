Poster and display icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 7 of the all-categories run: 8 products, 23 options. X-banners, cardboard roll-up banners and wall banners (complete set or the print only), wall banner widths, poster hangers (31 / 51 / 71 cm), poster frames (the profile, 25 / 32 mm), fence and construction fence banners (tie wraps), and the gazebo's print area (roof, back, side panels). On live most are line drawings in different styles (an orange banner, a black tent, frame corners in a circle); the cardboard set and the tie wraps are photos.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-20): my pick, built into icons/posters/, printarea/gazebo-* and live-opts/posters/. Our kit language for the shapes, the live photo for what you recognise by its look.
  • Set or print only (X-banner, wall banner): the product front-on as in our kits, minus the part you don't get. Complete = the print on its hardware, print only = the print alone (same view, so the row reads as "with / without"), frame only = the hardware alone. The X-banner frame is an X to the four corners with the hub and the feet, as live, front-on; in the complete set it shows dashed behind the canvas (hidden = dashed, as a fold). The wall banner hangs between two rods with end caps, as live.
  • Cardboard roll-up: B, the live photos. The complete set is the same photo as the cardboard stand on the roller banner page (one product, one picture), the print only is the rolled print. A (our old kit drawing) would bring back the icon we just replaced there.
  • Sizes are the same icon scaled (the kit rule): wall banners 60 / 80 the width only (the height is the same), poster hangers 31 / 51 / 71 the whole poster, the rails wood-coloured (the hanger is the product).
  • Poster frames: A, the corner close up with the aluminium profile in its width, as live but without the circle. In B (the whole frame) 25 and 32 mm differ by one pixel at 40.
  • Tie wraps: the live photo (hardware you recognise by its look), "Banner only" the dashed circle as "No extra options" on the signs. Drawn, a white tie wrap on a white banner disappears.
  • Gazebo: the print area rule: the tent front-on, the printed parts grey (roof with its valance, the back wall, the high side panels).
New: 3 X-banner + 3 wall banner (the complete set is the 80 cm icon) + 3 poster hanger + 2 poster frame + 3 gazebo icons; 4 photos (cardboard set = the stand photo we have, the rolled print, black and white tie wraps).
