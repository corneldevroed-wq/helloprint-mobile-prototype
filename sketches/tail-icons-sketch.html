The tail: one-off option icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 8, the last of the all-categories run: about 80 options, almost all of them one product each. I sorted them into three piles: options an icon we have already covers (mapped, nothing to draw), options where an icon adds nothing and the text says it all, and the ones worth a new icon or the live photo. Print technique (228 options) and labels on roll stay parked, as agreed.

My pick: folds A, balloons A, the rest as shown (one direction each, by the rules we already have).
  • Folds A: the profile from above, the C of a tri-fold and the Z of a z-fold, as our spine rule (the U from above). Opened flat (our fold icons) the two look the same: two dashed lines. B (half open, front-on) reads as a leaflet, not as a fold type.
  • Balloons A: the balloon with its curled ribbon or without (the set rule: the same view minus the part); B, "No finishing" as the dashed circle, says nothing about what you get.
  • New icons by the rules: entrance tickets (the perforation dashed, the serial "001" on the ticket and on the stub), NCR numbered (the sheet with "001"), binders (the spine U from above, 50 / 70 mm), notebook paper (plain, ruled, grid, dotted), paper cup lids (none, white, black: the colour is the choice), bamboo cutlery (fork, knife, spoon), the tablecloth hem (the folded band with its stitch), sunglasses print area (the arm side-on, left / right mirrored, both stacked), stickers on sheet, rainbow foil (the foil sheet, iridescent).
  • The live photo (hardware you recognise by its look): the five office stamps, the vboard double-sided tape and suction cups.
New: 29 icons, 7 photos; about 20 options mapped to icons we have, about 20 stay text.
