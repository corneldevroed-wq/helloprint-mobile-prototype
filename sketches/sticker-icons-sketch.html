Sticker icons

Sketch, not linked. The stickers wave from the gap analysis, for Labels on roll and Custom vinyl stickers. It is 15 icons, not 19: the vinyls are the same white and clear films as the labels (and the PVC-free vinyl is the white film with the eco leaf), the silver film reuses metallic-silver. Each direction is shown at 100 px and in the real option rows (40 mobile, 50 desktop), next to the icons it sits with. Grey block = a one-off icon from a later wave.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-16): shapes B, films A; the roll parts are parked for later. My pick was shapes A, films A, roll A: Shapes A is the size-shape language you chose for the folds (flat, white, one dark line), so round and oval sizes can simply be the same icon scaled, next to the plain rectangles. Films stay in the material family (sheet with a folded corner) so they sit calmly beside kraft and recycled; the checkerboard is the one sign for transparent everybody knows. Eco = a small light-green leaf, the only colour. Roll A is flat like the rest; the 3/4 cylinder (B) is more illustrated and gets busy at 40.
