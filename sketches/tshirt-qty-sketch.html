T-shirt quantities with prices

  • Now (prototype and live): you type the pieces per size (S–XXL); the total is the quantity. One price line: "€16.48 each now, €13.20 each at 25 pcs" (live: "Price per piece €20.27"). What 50 or 100 cost, you only find out by typing.
  • A: the size tiles stay first; under them the steps with their price each, yours in green, and a nudge to the next step ("15 more for €13.20 each").
  • B: first the quantity, as on every other product (the quantity list in 2 columns with each and total), then you spread it over the sizes ("6 of 10 placed").
  • C: the size tiles, and under them a small price table (quantity · each · total), your row highlighted.
  • The size and the field apart (Cornel): a real text field to type in (the checkout's, 44 high), the size under it; a size with pieces gets the green outline. In all three.
  • Type in the fields: the sketches follow (Recommended prices).

My pick: A. A t-shirt order starts with the people who wear them (how many S, M, L…), so the sizes stay first and the total follows; the steps show at a glance what more costs per piece, and the nudge turns that into an upsell. B makes you pick a number first and then fit the sizes to it (an extra rule to meet); C is clear but heavier.
