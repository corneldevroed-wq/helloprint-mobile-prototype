Print technique icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 2 of the all-categories run: "Print technique" in 81 products. 10 new icons: screen print, pad print, full colour (live calls digital print and digital transfer both "Full color", so one icon), transfer, DTF transfer, screen transfer, laser engraving (also "Engraving"), hot stamping, embossing / debossing, UV print. Reused: No imprint = the unprinted ink dot (inks/none), Screenround = screen print, Digital sticker = the sticker contour. Each direction at 100 px, as live pills and as option cards, in the rows of a bag, a pen and a notebook.

My pick: A, the tool. These options are processes, and their results look alike: screen print, pad print and a transfer all end as a flat logo, so B (the result) only differs in tiny details. The tool is different every time (the screen with the squeegee, the rubber pad, the print head with its four drops, the peeled transfer sheet, the laser beam, the foil and the stamp), and the ink stays the orange or CMYK we use for the inks. C is the clearest at once, but letters in a tile are a code to learn, not a picture.
