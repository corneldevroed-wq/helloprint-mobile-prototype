Brief: the product cards in the cart ("Our best sellers" in an empty cart, "Often ordered with" in a full one) put a preset in the cart with one click. The card has to say exactly what you add: size, paper, finishing, sides. Today it only says "500 pieces for €23.23".

The preset is each product's Recommended set (the one the PDP opens with); the price on the card is that set at that quantity, and Edit in the cart opens it on the PDP to change. Desktop shown; mobile gets the same text on its 2 × 2 cards.

A · One spec line under the name my pickThe preset in one short grey line (the same words as the cart line it becomes): "85 x 55 mm · Matt · Thick · Matte lamination". You see what you add without reading a list; the cards stay as calm as the home cards. Long sets are cut to the 4 choices that matter most.
B · The choices as a listThree or four ticks with the main choices. The clearest, it reads like "this is what's in the box", but every card gets 3 lines taller and the row gets busy.
C · One line + "What you get"Only the size on the card, a link opens all the choices (shown open on the first card). The calmest card, but the details are one click away, and that's exactly what the card has to tell.
