My account: Personal details

Cornel chose A (2026-11-18), with Password inside Profile and an icon per setting: built as prototype/personal-details.html. My pick: A. A settings list: a card per section (Profile, Communication preferences, Password, Company info, E-invoicing), every setting a row with what it is now and one small button on the right (Edit, Add, Change, Set up); Edit opens our standard popup with just those fields (shown open: Your name). The page stays calm and readable, you change one thing at a time, and on mobile the popup is a bottom sheet, as every edit in My account (your reference, a note). B is the artifact 1:1: Order details cards with “Edit details” that turn the card into fields in place: familiar, but the page jumps while you edit and the two-column grid gets cramped on mobile. C is one big form with one Save: quick to scan, but everything looks editable all the time, and a switch that works right away next to fields that wait for Save is confusing.
  • From the artifact: name, phone, email (order updates + sign-in codes), marketing emails and texts (switches, they work right away), password (last changed), company name, VAT number, invoice email (“each address can override these in Addresses”), e-invoicing per country.
  • Ours: Ireland first in the countries; one example set up (Peppol) so “Change” shows next to “Set up”.
  • Only UI guide parts: .dash-card + .dash-row.text, .btn small, .switch, .oc-od grid (B), .field, the popup.
