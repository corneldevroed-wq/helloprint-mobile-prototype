Brief: the desktop version of the checkout (Details → Payment → Confirmed). The mobile checkout is the basis: same blocks, same order, same wording. The mobile sticky bar (total + Continue / Place order) has to go somewhere on desktop.

Shown with the Details step as a guest. Payment uses the same frame (pay now / later, wallets, card; "Place order" in place of "Continue to payment"). Confirmed gets the same header and its own content, no sidebar.

Two questions this sketch answers:

  • Header: the full shop header (search, menu) or a focused checkout header (logo, steps, Secure Checkout), like the mobile checkout and most shops?
  • Where do the total and the button go: a sticky summary card like the desktop cart, or under the form like mobile?
A · Two columns like the cart, focused header my pick The mobile checkout header on desktop, steps in the middle. Left: the mobile form (max 640 wide, fields in pairs as on mobile). Right: the cart's sticky "Order overview" card, with the products, the totals, the button and the delivery date. A small footer (legal), no shop footer.
  • + Same grid and card as the desktop cart (1fr + 392, gap 32): the cart → details step feels like one page that moves on.
  • + The total and the button are always in view (the job of the mobile sticky bar).
  • + No search or menu: fewer ways out of the checkout. Logo and bag still go back.
  • − The product lines are in the card twice over the steps (cart + checkout), but small.
