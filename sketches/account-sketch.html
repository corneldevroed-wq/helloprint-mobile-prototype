Same content in all three, only the way around differs. The Overview, in this order: Needs your attention (our to-do blocks of order confirmed, Pay always first), Order again second (Cornel 2026-11-17: re-orders matter most to us; only what has a deadline goes above it: Reorder, change options, download proof), Orders in progress (status + where it is: Artwork · Proof · Printing · Delivery), Invoices and Quotes side by side (the last ones with their status, the open total + Pay all, Request a quote), and Previously viewed (our product cards, 4 in a row). Everything of the wishes' Overview is in; Recent orders became Orders in progress (with where each order is). Complaints is its own page (Cornel), the VIP score stays.
My pick: A. The sidebar gives a fixed place on desktop and shows where you are on every account page; on mobile there is no room for it, so the Overview ends in the same rows (the account menu of the header) and every sub page gets ← Account Overview. B's tabs get crowded with 7 pages and scroll on mobile; C is calm but costs a click to reach your orders.
  • Reused as is: the to-do block (.oc-act), the account menu rows (.d-menu.d-head), .pill, .icon-btn + tooltip, .tabs, .crumbs, the label (.badge.eco).
  • New (to the design system once chosen): the account card, the status chip (only "Action needed" in colour, the rest a dot + grey text), the progress tracker (the look of the checkout steps; .co-steps itself is too tied to the checkout), the VIP bar, and in C the tiles.
  • The account menu in the header gets Complaints in place of Support (Support stays under Need help?).
