Black Friday page: page type, products and style

Black Friday is Friday 27 November, Cyber Monday 30 November. Live en-ie has two pages for it: /black-friday ("Prepare for Black Friday": print for the customer's own Black Friday) and /deals (our own offer with a code, 15% off selected items). One page replaces both.

1 · Page type: a PLP

A Black Friday visitor comes from an email, an ad or the site notification with one question: what is cheaper and by how much? The PLP answers that fastest: products with their old and new price straight under the intro, tabs to jump to a group, one click to the PDP. It is built exactly as Promotional Print (our PLP): title + intro, tabs, promo card + categories, product rows, a content row, reviews, FAQ, SEO text. No marketing hero, no new block: the campaign lives in the intro, the promo cards and the product photos.

TypeWhat it is good atWhy not for Black Friday
PLP my pickMany products with prices across groups, tabs to jump, the PDP one click away(fits)
CLPOne product family explained (types, materials, sizes)Black Friday is across the range; a CLP is one family
Marketing pageA story or a theme (about us, rugby clubs, storm flags)Long copy, 64px rhythm, products halfway down: made for reading, not for a deal

Two conversion choices that go with it: the price is already reduced on the card (old price crossed out), no code to copy at checkout; and the end date in the intro ("until Monday 30 November, 23:59"), no ticking countdown: the same urgency, no kermis.

Not on the page: products without a discount, and quotes (live excludes them too). Deal conditions go in the FAQ.

3 · Style: the products themselves are Black Friday

Every product gets a new transparent cutout with one Black Friday design printed on it: matte black, "BLACK FRIDAY" in white condensed capitals, one orange (our --orange) price tag. The same design on every product, so the rows read as one campaign, and it shows the customer what their own Black Friday print could look like. Because the cutout is transparent, the tile is a CSS choice: our grey tile (A) or a black one (B).

To check before the build: the mug and tote are shown black; that is only honest if we sell them in black (full-colour print on the mug, a black tote colour).
