Design cockpit

The idea: the design options leave the bottom of the PDP and get a page of their own. The PDP ends with one button, Continue to your design (price and delivery stay). Then: PDP → Add your design → cart.
  • Round 2, only UI guide parts, nothing new except B’s preview tile: the shell, .ha-back, the checkout layout (.co-page: 608 + 64 + 392 on desktop), .co-head, the payment methods list (.pm: radio rows, the chosen one opens in place), the upload field from the Upload your artwork popup (.art-drop, .up-file, .art-calm) with the PDP’s template download (.d-tpl-dl), the cart’s product line (.line-item.cart-line + .eta), the Order overview card (.cart-side), the sticky bar (.cart-bar), .addr-list and .tracker (C).
  • Add to cart stays grey until the design is settled: a file in, Canva done, or Upload later chosen.
  • Prototype data: Standard flyers, 1,000 pcs, A5, Glossy, €38.01 excl. VAT.
