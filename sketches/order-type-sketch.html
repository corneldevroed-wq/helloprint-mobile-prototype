Brief (Cornel, 2026-11-06): we keep guessing whether someone is a consumer or a business. Ask it at the top of the checkout, under Contact details, as bol does (Particulier / Zakelijk). Business adds the fields we have now (company name, VAT number) plus a reference number (PO / reference for their own administration; in the backlog since November).

Each option: the Personal state, the Business state, and logged in (Business). Mobile and desktop show the same block.

A · A toggle on top: Personal | Business my pick The two-way toggle we already use for Pay now | Pay later (.seg), first thing under the title, Personal on by default. Business slides in the three fields under the email; the reference number has one line why.
  • + One look, one tap: the question is asked before anything else, and it is the same control as on payment.
  • + Small: no extra cards, no labels; the page stays calm for the 80% who are consumers.
  • − A toggle doesn't explain what Business gives you; the fields that appear do that.
B · Two option cards with a line each Two .opt cards side by side (as the PDP options): Personal "For yourself" and Business "VAT invoice, your reference". Business opens the same fields.
  • + Explains the difference before you choose; closest to bol.
  • − Two cards at the top of the form is heavier than the question deserves.
C · The checkbox, moved to the top "I'm ordering for a business" stays a checkbox, but now directly under the title (above the email), with the reference number added.
  • + The smallest change.
  • − A checkbox reads as an extra, not as a question everyone answers: the guessing stays.
D · Section tabs: Personal | Business The section tabs from the UI guide (.tabs): semibold grey, the active one black with the 2px light green line, on the light grey line. Directly under the title, Personal active by default; Business opens the same fields.
  • + Light and calm: just two words on a line, no box; the most "web" of the four.
  • − In the guide these tabs jump to a section of a long page (navigation); here they would choose a setting of the order, so the same look gets a second meaning. The login page does the same with Log in | Register, so people know it.

My pick: A. It asks the question everyone has to answer, at the top, with a control people already know from the payment step, and it costs one line for consumers. The business fields do the explaining when they appear. D is my second: lighter than A, but those tabs mean "go to a section" everywhere else; A reads as "choose one".

The reference number only for business, optional, with "Shown on your invoice and packing slip". If consumers ask for it too, it can move out of the business block later. On payment and confirmed the order type, company and reference come back in the overview.
