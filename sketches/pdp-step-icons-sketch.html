Icons for the new PDP steps

Sketch, not linked. Asked by the PDP session (11-22): an audit of the 29 PDPs found seven steps that show text only (cards without an icon row): pages (stapled booklets, perfect bound brochures), roll winding, core diameter and dispenser (labels on roll), number of sheets (notepads), holes (Foamex signs) and laser engraving (Ebony pens). The live drawings are above each group; pages and sheets have none on live (a dropdown).

Chosen (Cornel, 11-23): my pick, built into icons/labels/, panels/holes-* and inks/laser; the roll winding redrawn in the live shape (the roll top left, the web hanging down). Pages and sheets stay text.
  • Roll winding: as live, in our line: the roll from its end, the web with two labels, the A shows which edge comes off first. Live's drawing is the clearest we found for this; there is no better way to say 0 / 90 / 180 / 270°.
  • Core: the same roll from its side, only the core hole changes (40 / 76 mm), as live but without the numbers (the label says them).
  • Dispenser A: the set rule, the roll alone or in its box with a label out of the slot. B (the live photos) are product shots with a printed logo, loud next to icons, and a box is a box: nothing to recognise.
  • Holes: the panel front-on with its holes, none = the plain panel (the set rule), as live. Not notepads/holes-* (ring binder holes along an edge).
  • Laser engraving A: it sits in the inks row (full colour, 1–4 colours, unprinted), so it is an ink dot too: silver, the metal showing through, with engraved lines. B (the laser beam) is the tool from the parked print technique sketch; if you choose "the tool" there, this becomes B everywhere.
  • Pages and sheets B: text. 15 booklet steps (8–64) and 12 brochure steps (28–400) only differ by a hair as a drawing; the number is the information (the rule of 11-20: calendar sheets and NCR sets per pad stay text). A shows what that would look like: a spine U per step, the notepad block thicker.
New (my pick): 4 winding + 2 core + 2 dispenser + 3 holes + 1 laser engraving = 12 icons.
