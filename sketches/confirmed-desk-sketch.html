My pick: A. The customer just pressed Pay in this exact frame; the confirmed page should feel like the last step of it, not a new page. The one task (the upload) gets the wide column right under the thank you, and the overview card stays where the eye already knows it. When no file is needed, the upload simply drops out and the status moves up.
