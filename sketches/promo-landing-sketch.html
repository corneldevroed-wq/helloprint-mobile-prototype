Promo landing page: "Storm damage? New flag."

One message, one action. Someone whose flag tore in the storm wants three things fast: the right flag, the date it arrives, and no hassle. Everything on the page serves that; everything else (menu tabs, other categories, blogs) goes. The same template then carries Christmas, Back to school, Black Friday… only the md changes.

Which template

A · The category template

Works already (Hospitality). But tabs, 8 sections and dozens of tiles: lots to browse, no single message. Good for SEO traffic, weak for someone who clicked an ad with one problem.

B · A new promo template My pick

Hero with the message + date, one product choice, why it holds, 3 steps, proof, FAQ, the CTA again; a sticky CTA on mobile. Mostly existing components; 3 small new ones. Fits every campaign.

C · Straight to a PDP with a storm banner

Fastest, but it picks the flag for them (and a storm hits mast, facade and feather flags alike). The banner gets skipped. Fine as an ad target for one product, not for the message.
