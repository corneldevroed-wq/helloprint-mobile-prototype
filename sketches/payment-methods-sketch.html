B · One list, the chosen method opens my pick One outlined group (as the reference): a row per method with its radio, name and logos. The chosen row opens a light grey panel under it with what that method needs: the card fields, or one line for a wallet ("You'll pay in the Apple Pay window").
  • + Every method looks equal and reads as one choice; scales to more methods per market (Klarna, iDEAL) without new buttons.
  • + Calm: no black / yellow brand buttons on the page, just small logos; the panel uses our light grey block (as Business).
  • − Apple / Google Pay lose their "one tap" button look; the pay button in the sidebar does the job.
C · A card per method The same rows as separate cards with 8 between; the chosen card gets the 2px green border (our radio row rule) and opens its fields.
  • + Follows the radio row rule to the letter (address cards, quantity rows).
  • − Four boxes plus the field boxes inside: busier than one group.

My pick: B. It turns four different-looking things (three brand buttons, an OR, a card box) into one calm choice, with the details only for the method you picked. It also grows per market without changing the layout. The light grey panel is the same block as the business fields on details, so it is nothing new.
