Fold and envelope icons

Sketch, not linked. The first fold and envelope icons were not nice (skewed panels, grey inside, a card poking out of an envelope). PMS stays as it is. Three directions each, shown at 100 px, in the mobile option card (40) and the desktop card (50), next to the size shapes they sit with.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-16): folds A + envelopes B. Folds A is the size shape itself (flat outline) with the fold as a dashed line, so a folded size reads as "this shape, folded here", sits calmly in a row of plain size shapes and scales per size like the other shapes. B (standing V) is the most illustrated, but it stands out in a row of flat shapes; C gets busy at 40. Envelopes B: the closed envelope says it in one look, and Without is the card alone. A (stacked like Double-sided) is more literal but busier at 40; C is clip art.
