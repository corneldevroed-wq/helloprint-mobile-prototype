Print colour icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 1 of the all-categories run: "Amount of colours in your design" in 111 merchandise products (pens, bags, notebooks, lanyards…): Unprinted, 1–5 colours, Full colour. Live shows it as a pill with a small icon, so each direction is shown as a pill too. The inks are the PMS dots we have (orange, light green, dark green), then blue and dark grey for 4 and 5; full colour = the four CMYK dots. At the bottom the print-product extras (black, PMS 4, full colour with a black back), built on the printing icons.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-17): A, only the ink dots. My reasons: The question is "how many colours", and a row of dots counts them at a glance, also as a 32 px pill icon. It is the PMS language without the sheet: on a pen or a bag there is no sheet. B puts a paper sheet on products that are not paper; C (a logo) is the nicest idea but at 32 px the 4 and 5 colour logos turn into confetti, and the reader has to count parts instead of dots.
