The tail, whole menu

Sketch, not linked. Wave 5 of the whole-menu run (sketches/icon-gaps-whole-menu.html): the one-offs, about 40 options, one or a few products each. Sorted by our rules: a shape gets an icon in our line, hardware you recognise by its look gets the live photo, a foil gets the foil sheet, and a few stay text.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-23): built as sketched (icons in gadgets/, stickers/, folders/, exhibition/, foils in materials/, photos in live-opts/tail/). One direction, the rules we have.
  • Icons (the set rule: the same view with or without the part): takeaway bowl with / without lid, sticker for a flat / curved surface (the sticker grey on the line, as live), confetti flower / heart, the ring binder opened flat (outside: window on the back, grip hole; inside: the rings, 2 or 4, window inside; both = two stacked), the high table with its stretch cover or the cover alone, a cushion or beanbag filled (the dots, as live) or the case alone.
  • Foil sheets for the stampings and foils: copper, black, white, dark red (gold and silver we have).
  • The live photo: chalk pens 3 / 15 mm, white magnets (with the three we have), tape dispensers standard / deluxe, parasol bases white / black, the A-board top card, the 10 x 10 hanging plate. Poster foil = the clear film icon we have.
  • Text: lighting 24 W, edged glass, double-sided wall tape, the music card songs.
