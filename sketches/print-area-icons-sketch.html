Print area icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 3 of the all-categories run: "Print area" in 113 merchandise products. On live every product has its own picture; the option is a size plus a place (the front, both sides, the top of the cover, next to the clip, the strap, the card holder). Here: one drawing per product type with the area in it, and the size stays text. 15 icons: bag (large, small, each one or both sides), notebook (full cover, large, small), pen (barrel, clip), lanyard (strap, holder, each one or both sides), mug (wrap, one side). Unprinted = the unprinted ink dot. The gazebo and the sunglasses arms (one product each) are left for the tail.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-17): A. My reasons: The product as a flat outline with the area in grey is the language you chose for the napkin print areas, so it is one rule for all print areas. B (a dashed print zone on a light outline) looks like a technical drawing and goes faint at 40. C (no product, only the share of a surface) needs just 6 icons for everything, but a pen strip and a lanyard strap become the same bar, and you lose what you are looking at.
