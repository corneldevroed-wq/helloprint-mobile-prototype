My account: what goes in front of a to-do

Cornel chose A (2026-11-18), then moved the icon into the green button instead (built that way). My pick was B. A big light green circle (40) with what you have to do in it: € to pay, an eye for a proof, the upload arrow, a wrench in red for a fix. It is the first thing you see, it tells you what kind of task it is before you read, and it has the weight of the to-do (the done tick grows with it, so the column stays). A keeps the old size with the task icon: tidy, but small, as the numbers were. C puts one “!” in front of every task: important, but it says the same thing four times and nothing about what. Only from the UI guide: the action block, the small buttons, the cream band, Material Symbols (the icon set of the guide), --light-green and --error.
