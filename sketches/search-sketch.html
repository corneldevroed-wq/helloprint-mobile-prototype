New page type: Search results (test: “pens”)

Live example: helloprint.com/en-ie/search?q=pens (2,921 results) and ?q=pos. Here the 181 live pens of the PFP, so the filters work.

What goes wrong for the customer on live today

  • Broken text: a card says “High quality {{product_name}}”, the title “111 Results for " pos "” has spaces in the quotes.
  • The same navigation twice: “Related pages” pills and a “Categories” filter, mostly the same names. Which one do you use?
  • Odd hits: “pos” finds Tony’s Chocolonely and Cards & Invitations (part of a word matches). Relevance has to win over substring.
  • Two price models mixed: “From €9.58” next to “€77.49 each”: you can’t compare. No delivery on the card, while that is why people pick.
  • Only one filter: “pens” gives 2,921 results and you can only tick “Pens”. No type, delivery, price, colour, eco.
  • Pages 1–5 + Next instead of “Show more”. A second big search field under the header’s search field.
  • No results: emoji categories and two lists of the same six words; no “did you mean”.

In all options: it is the PFP

  • Same page type, same script (pfp.js): sticky filter sidebar (a sheet on mobile), filters work at once with counts, soft tags, “Show 24 more”, the card with “500 pcs for” + each, the colour strip and “Fast delivery”.
  • New is only: the title with the query, Sort with Relevance first (the default), and where the categories go. No reviews, FAQ or SEO text (a search page is noindex).
  • One price model: every card “N pcs for” at one quantity, as on the PFP. Products without a quantity price (the “€77.49 each” ones) say “1 pc for”.

My pick: B. Search is a shortcut to a list, so it should feel exactly like the list: the header field holds your query (no second field), the title says what you searched, and the categories become the first filter “Product type”, as “Pen type” on the PFP. One place to narrow down instead of two. C adds the category tiles on top: nice when the word is a category (“posters”), but for “pens” they push the products a screen down and repeat the filter.
