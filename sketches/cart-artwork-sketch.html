Brief: after "Upload your artwork" on the PDP the cart line shows the customer's own file instead of the product photo (as the old site did), and hovering it shows it bigger. The question: what if they upload 2, 3 or 10 files?

Every option: 1 file / 2 files (front + back) / 4 files; the middle card shows the hover. Mobile has no hover: a tap on the tile opens the same files in a sheet. Not uploaded yet (Upload after checkout, Canva): the product photo, as now.

A · A pile my pickThe first file on top, the next ones peek out behind it (max two edges) and a small "4 files" chip. You still see the design clearly and you feel there's more. Hover: all files side by side at a readable size, with their names.
B · Split tileTwo files: front and back next to each other; four or more: a 2 × 2 grid, "+N" on the last. Shows everything at once, but every file gets small, and a business card front is hard to read at 50px. Hover: as A.
C · First file + countOnly the first file, as one upload, with a "+3" chip. The calmest tile. Hover: the first file big, the others as small thumbnails under it to switch. The others are one step further away.
