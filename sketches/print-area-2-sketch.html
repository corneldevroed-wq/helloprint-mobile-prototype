Print area: the new product types

Sketch, not linked. Wave 1 of the whole-menu run (sketches/icon-gaps-whole-menu.html): ≈ 230 products whose print area step names a position on a product type we have not drawn: bottle, mug (handed, handle, back), umbrella, backpack, cooler bag, duffel / travel bag, drawstring bag, paper bag, tech. Sizes stay text, as in our first print area set; a step that only lists sizes keeps its text cards.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-23): A, one base per product type in our print area style; built into icons/printarea/.
  • A: the product flat and front-on in our line, the print area grey, as bag-large, mug-small and pen-barrel today. One base per type, so a position reads the same on every bottle or backpack, whatever the brand. Both sides = two stacked (the rule we have). New: the back = the area with a dashed edge, lighter grey: the side you do not see, as the frame hidden behind an X-banner. The umbrella from above, eight panels, panel 1 towards you.
  • B: live's own drawings (above each group). There is one per product and option, so they are exact (the area at its real size), but each in another style: black or grey areas, thick or thin lines, some with a printed design. Next to our icons that is loud, and 600 images to keep.
New (A): 28 icons: bottle 3, mug 5, umbrella 2, backpack 4, cooler bag 4, duffel 4, drawstring 1, paper bag 3, tech 2.
