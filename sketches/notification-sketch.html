Site notification: where?

A service message for the whole site (an outage, a delay at a carrier, a holiday cut-off). One sentence with one link, a × to close; once closed it stays away for the session. It scrolls away with the page (not sticky). The real home page below, mobile 390 and desktop 1512 (shown at 2/3). My pick: A, above the header: a message for the whole site is read first there, it keeps the header (logo, search, categories) one block, and it stays clear of the mega menu and the search dropdown that open under the category bar. Live en-gb has it at the bottom: easy to miss, and on mobile it clashes with the sticky bars (PDP, checkout).
