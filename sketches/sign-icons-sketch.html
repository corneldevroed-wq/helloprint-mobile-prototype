Sign and panel material icons

Sketch, not linked. Wave 5 of the all-categories run: the sign and panel materials (27 products, 66 options: aluminium, indoor and outdoor signs, eco signs, Correx and Foamex signs, panels, photo on aluminium / Forex / plexiglass, backdrops, window stickers, whiteboards). On live every material is a corner photo of the panel, so you see its edge: the Correx flutes, the Reboard honeycomb, the Dibond core, the plexi thickness. That edge is what tells these materials apart; their faces are nearly all white.

Chosen: D, the live photo redrawn as an icon (Cornel, 11-18; built into icons/panels/). The panel from above with its corner towards you, as on live, both front edges showing the core; Dibond thin, Reboard thick. It reads as a sign at once, it keeps what customers know from live, and it adds the thickness that B could not show. It is our first icon at an angle; I think that is right here, because the thickness is the choice (as the angle of a wall mount). My earlier pick was B.
  • A (the material sheet with the folded corner) says paper or film: a panel does not fold, and the faces of Dibond, Forex, Easyprint, Sign Again and cardboard are all white, so the row is seven white sheets.
  • B (my earlier pick) keeps our flat material language (same size, same light outline, the face texture) but squares the corners and adds the edge strip with the core. Seven white faces, seven different edges. Rule: flexible = the sheet with the folded corner, rigid = the panel with its edge.
  • D is C without its noise: one crop, no printed designs, our outline and textures.
  • C (the live photos) reads well big, but each is a different crop with a different printed design on it (yellow stripes, blue drops), loud next to our icons; at 44 the edges disappear.
  • Sign Again Uncoated and Akylite now use the paper icon "recycled"; in B they become panels too (the recycled specks on the face), so the row stays one family.
Not a choice: four new flexible materials as the usual sheet, six mapped to icons we have, and the hardware (hanging plates, spacer, ground plates, whiteboard kit) as the live photo, as the flag bases.
