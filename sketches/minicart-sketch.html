Mini cart. Live (en-ie, looked at 2026-11-23): Add to cart and the bag icon open a drawer from the right over a blurred page: "Your Cart · 2 items", every line (photo, specs, qty, delivery, Edit, bin), the totals with the discount, Proceed to Checkout and Continue Shopping. The point: add, see it landed, shop on.

Below: 1 when it opens (per design choice), 2 desktop, 3 mobile. Prototype data, the pages behind are blurred stand-ins.

1 · When does it open?

Where the line comes fromAfter it lands in the cartWhy
PDP · Upload laterMini cart opens, you stay on the PDPThe classic case: nothing else to do now, shop on.
PDP · Upload your artworkMini cart opens (the line shows the artwork tile)The files were uploaded in the popup on the PDP: done, the same as Upload later.
PDP · Design online (Canva) / logo editorStraight to the cart page, no mini cartA separate tool: you come back from it with a finished design; the cart page is where you land, as live. Going back to the PDP to open a drawer would be a strange detour.
Cart · Edit a line → Update cartBack to the cart page (toast "Your cart is updated")You came from the cart; you go back to it.
Cart · Often ordered with (+ Add)Stays on the cart page, the line appears (toast)The cart is already the full cart.
Bag icon, cart not emptyDesktop: mini cart · Mobile: see 3Peek without leaving the page.
Bag icon, cart emptyThe cart page (empty state with best sellers)An empty drawer has nothing to say; the empty cart page does.

2 · Desktop

A · As live: the whole cart

Every line the same, the totals and Checkout. Familiar, but after Add to cart you have to look for what you just added, and with 5 lines the drawer is a long list.

B · What you added on top my pick

"Added to your cart" with the new line in a light green tint on top, the other lines under it (compact, no options), the total and two buttons: Go to checkout and Continue shopping. From the bag icon the same drawer without the "Added" part. Confirms the add, and still shows the whole cart.

3 · Mobile

A · The drawer, full width

As live: the whole cart over the whole screen. On a phone that is the cart page again, in a layer, with less room: two places for the same thing.

B · A bottom sheet "Added to your cart" my pick

Our standard mobile popup: the added line, "2 items · €75.99", Go to checkout and Continue shopping (closes). Thumb-friendly, swipe down to close, the PDP stays under it. The bag icon on mobile goes to the cart page (the phone’s full cart), as now.

C · Only a toast

A bar "Added to your cart · View cart" for 4 seconds, then gone. The calmest, but easy to miss, and no way to checkout without finding the bag.
