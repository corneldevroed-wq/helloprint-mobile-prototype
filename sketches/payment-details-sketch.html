Brief (payment review, points 6 + 7): just before paying you can't see who orders, where it ships, or on whose name the invoice goes. Show the details from the previous step, each with a way back to change it. A business order also shows the company and the reference number (they go on the invoice and the packing slip).

Example: business order for Murphy Print Ltd, reference PO-2026-118, shipping to Grandma, billing to Office. Mobile shows the same block in the same place (A, C) or above the order overview (B).

A · A "Your details" block on top of payment my pick The first section of the page, before "When do you want to pay?": one card (the contact card of details) with three rows, a grey label, the details, and a "Change" link back to details.
  • + You check everything right before you pay, in the order of the page before; the reference sits with the company.
  • + Built from parts we have (the contact card, the text link); the same on mobile.
  • − The payment choice moves down a block (about 160 on desktop).

My pick: A. It answers the three questions people have before paying (is it me, does it go to the right place, is the invoice right) where they look first, each with its own Change back to the exact part of details. It reuses the contact card, so nothing new to build, and works the same on mobile. The reference number sits with the company, as on the invoice.

Rows that are "as usual" stay short: billing = shipping shows "Same as shipping address"; a personal order has no company line.
