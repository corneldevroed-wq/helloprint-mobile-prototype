Brief (Cornel, 2026-11-06): changing the shipping or the billing address feels off. Today a tap on an address chooses it and closes the popup, while the only (green) button is "New shipping address": you look for a button to agree and there isn't one. The billing popup adds a "Same as shipping address" card on top of the same address book, so the address you ship to is in it twice, and its button still says "New shipping address". It has to be flawless and intuitive.

Example: shipping to Grandma (the default), billing the same. Same content on mobile (a sheet from the bottom, drawn here 390 wide) and desktop (the same popup, 560, in the middle). Menus (⋮ Edit / Make default / Delete) stay as they are.

A · Choose in the list, one button confirms my pick A tap only moves the radio; Use this address confirms and closes; ✕ closes without changing anything. "+ New address" is the last item of the list (dashed, like the card on the page), so adding never competes with confirming. Billing works the same from the same address book: no separate "Same as shipping" card, the address you ship to has a small "Shipping address" label. On the page the checkbox goes; a Billing Address block shows the choice with Change.
  • + One primary action per popup, as everywhere in the checkout. Shipping and billing are the same pattern.
  • + No address twice. The page says in words what you chose ("Same as shipping address").
  • − One tap more than today to change the address (choose + confirm). Worth it: nothing changes by accident.
B · Two buttons in the footer As A, but the footer has two buttons: New address (outline) and Use this address (primary). Billing keeps the "Same as shipping address" card on top and the checkbox on the page.
  • + Both actions always in view.
  • − Two buttons side by side: a choice between two actions at the moment you only want to say "yes, this one".
  • − The double meaning in billing (Same as shipping + Grandma) stays.
C · A tap chooses and closes (as today), clearer No footer button: a tap on an address is the choice and closes the popup (as bol). "+ New address" is the last item of the list. Billing as in A (one address book, "Shipping address" label, a Billing Address block on the page).
  • + The fewest taps.
  • − Nothing to agree with: a mis-tap changes your address and closes; you only see it on the page.
  • − The popup does two jobs (manage with ⋮ and choose by tapping) without saying which tap does what.

My pick: A. It is the one people know from every good checkout: you choose, you confirm, ✕ means "nothing changed". One green button per popup tells you what it does. And billing stops being a special case: it is the same list, the same button, and "same as shipping" is simply the address you ship to, labelled.

On the page nothing else changes: tapping a card in the row still chooses it straight away (you see what you choose). The popup is for more: all addresses, edit, delete, add.
