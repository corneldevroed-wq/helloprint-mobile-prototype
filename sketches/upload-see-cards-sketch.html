Upload & See: the upgrades’ card on every decision

In the flow the upgrades read well because each decision is one block: the customer’s own flyer on the grey stage, fused with the text, one title that says what it is about, the consequence, the actions right under it. Here the same card on the other decision moments, now next to with cards. The order bar stays the anchor for the total. Test case: the landscape file, back “same design”.

The decision card

  • One card per decision: the grey stage left (on mobile on top) with the customer’s flyer showing the effect, the text right: a title (what changes, “Landscape file → portrait flyer”), the state with a tick or an orange dot, ticks for what you get.
  • The actions under the card, as wide as their words: buttons for a step, pills for a choice. The order bar keeps the total and the next step.
  • Only on decision moments. The team line, “Back to …” and the note link stay plain lines, so the page doesn’t turn into a stack of cards.
  • New in the design system if you choose it: the decision card (stage + text + actions), with a row in the UI guide. Later maybe elsewhere (the upload to-do on order confirmed, the cart’s upsell).
