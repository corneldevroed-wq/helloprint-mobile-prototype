Envelope icons: window, closure, window sticker side

Sketch, not linked. Wave 6 of the all-categories run: 6 products, 23 options. Envelopes (standard, unprinted, PMS, all-over, recycled): Window (without, left, right) and Closure (gummed, self-adhesive strips). Window stickers: which side the glue is on (front adhesive, back adhesive; both read from outside). On live these are already line drawings (below), not photos: a shape and a side, so an icon (the flag rule). Live's file names for left and right are swapped; the drawings match the options.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-19): A for the window, B for the closure, A for the window stickers; built into icons/envelopes/. My pick was A, A, B; the notes below are that reasoning.
  • Window A is live's drawing in our line: the envelope front-on with the flap peeking over the top, the window grey (the see-through part, as the grey print area). The flap is what makes a plain rectangle an envelope; B takes our "with envelope" icon (the V on the back) but the window sits on the front, so "without" (V) and the windows (no V) are two different views in one row.
  • Closure A shows the same envelope from the back with the flap open: the gum band grey, the strip with its liner peeling off. B is live's close-up of the flap corner: bigger detail, but at 40 it is an abstract corner, not an envelope.
  • Window stickers B is the cut through the window: the glass a grey bar, the sticker on the inside or outside face, the glue dashed against the glass, the eye outside. Both options read "visible outside", the only difference is where the glue is, and that only shows in a cut. A (live's front view, glare over the sticker = behind the glass) is a subtle cue that vanishes at 40.
