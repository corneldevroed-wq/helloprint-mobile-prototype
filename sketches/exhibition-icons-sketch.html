Wave 3 of the whole-menu run (sketches/icon-gaps-whole-menu.html): ≈ 45 products (pop-up and zipper walls, zipper banners, tube stands, counters, lightboxes, L-banners, U-walls, menu boards). Their steps: what you get (complete set / frame only / print only, 20 products), the base of a roller banner, lighting, shelf, magnets, pocket, connector, and single / double sided (we have that: printing/e-single|double). Live mixes four styles here: line drawings, black silhouettes, product photos and orange renders.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-23): B and A, the live photo for the hardware; built into icons/exhibition/ and live-opts/exhibition/. The four families get their own icons (you see the difference), every other display the trio.
  • B, one trio for every display: complete set = the print on its frame with the feet, frame only = the tubes (heavier, as our flag poles), print only = the print with its corner peeling (our sign for a print or a film, as live's canvas only). The choice in this step is the same in all 20 products: with or without the hardware. The product's shape is in its photo already; 15 different shapes would mean 45 icons, most used once.
  • A, per family (straight wall, curved wall, tube stand, counter) is closer to the product and follows the X-banner rule, but covers only these four; the zipper banners, lightboxes, L-banners and U-walls would each need their own three.
  • Hardware: the live photo (the rule we have): the roller banner bases (basic, deluxe, premium, triangle, as the stands), the lamp, the shelf, magnets, the A4 pocket, the connector; none = the dashed circle.
