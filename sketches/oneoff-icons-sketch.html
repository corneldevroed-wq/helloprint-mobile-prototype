One-off material icons

Sketch, not linked. The last wave from the gap analysis: materials that appear in one or two products. They join the material family (the sheet with the folded corner). 8 new icons, not 11: Neon Yellow is the existing neon.svg, and the PVC cards can keep glossy and silk-matte (B). Shown at 100 px and in the real option rows (40 mobile, 50 desktop), next to the materials they sit with.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-16): neon B, photo paper A (DOT foil and backlit A with it), antique and Paperwise white as shown, PVC cards A. My pick was:
  • Neon A: flat, as the neon yellow we already have; a glow (B) is the only effect in the whole family.
  • Photo paper A: the small photo tells it from glossy at once; B is just glossy. DOT foil A: a fine grid reads as glue dots, B looks like a pattern. Backlit A: the light in the sheet stays inside the icon; B with rays is kermis.
  • PVC cards B: they are the only option step on that product, glossy and matte say it all, and no new icon.
