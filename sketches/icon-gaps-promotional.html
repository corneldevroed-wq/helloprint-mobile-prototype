Funnel icons: what we miss for Promotional printing

Analysis, not linked. Source: every link on the live en-ie promotional-printing page (16 Nov 2026). 49 links: 27 are product funnels (counted below), 22 are category pages (listed at the end, for the next run). Every option of every step was read from the live funnel config and checked against our icons in design-system/icons (style: sketches/material-icons.html).

81% of the options that show a picture are covered today. 8 of the 27 products are complete already (flyers, eco and special flyers, placemats, posters blueback, coasters, perfect bound booklets, plantable postcards); 5 more need only 1 or 2 icons (classic business cards, loyalty cards, vouchers, white PVC cards, stapled booklets).

My pick: the order to draw them

  1. Cards wave, 12 icons: the 4 folds (greeting cards, invitations, half-fold leaflets, folded business and loyalty cards), envelope with/without (4 products) and the 6 PMS print-colour icons (compliment slips, notepads). Reaches 8 products; the folds also replace the old green-stroke size-folded-*.svg.
  2. Stickers wave, 19 icons: the 5 sticker shapes (also the round/oval sizes, scaled), 9 films and vinyls, 5 labels-on-roll parts. Labels on roll is the biggest gap of all (38 options).
  3. Product kits, 31 icons: roller banner (5), napkins (11), notepads and booklets (7), folders (5), table talkers (2), A-board (1). Each is one product, so draw a kit when that PDP is next.
  4. One-off materials, 11 icons: the 4 neon colours (neon posters + posters), photo paper, DOT foil, backlit, antique white, Paperwise white, and the 2 PVC cards (or reuse glossy / silk-matte for those).

Kept as code, not new SVGs: every flat size (a rectangle in proportion, also posters, notepads, napkins, coasters), and every gsm step (the thickness line). Roll winding is one drawing turned 0/90/180/270. Sizes of folders, roller banners and A-boards are one icon scaled per size, like the banner sizes.

The 73 new icons

grey = still to draw. Done 16 Nov: the cards wave, the stickers wave without the roll parts (the vinyls and the silver film reuse the films), the product kits and the one-off materials. Still to draw: the 5 labels-on-roll parts (parked)
