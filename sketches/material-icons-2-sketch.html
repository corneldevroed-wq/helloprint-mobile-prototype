Most of their materials are icons we have under another product name (Dibond, brushed aluminium, plexiglass, Correx, films, polyesters, papers). New are the floor and wall foils, a few panels and the mug body. Live shows each as a photo of the material with a printed design peeling off (loud next to our sheets).

Chosen (Cornel, 11-23): built into icons/materials/ and icons/panels/. One direction, our two material languages.
  • Flexible = the sheet with the folded corner, its face the texture you would feel: asphalt foil (anti-skid grit), Roughmark (the rough embossed face for walls), suction cup foil (rings, bigger than the DOT foil's dots), ceramic (white glaze with its sheen), stoneware (matte, warm, speckled).
  • Rigid = the panel's corner with its edge (sign panels D): Trespa (HPL, a dark brown core), Dibond double-sided (two stacked, our both-sides rule), honeycomb board in kraft (Reboard with a kraft face).
  • Mapped, nothing to draw: white polymer for vehicles and white high tack = the white film (the glue and the years are in the label), High Definition Print = glossy polyester (live uses the same picture), Airtex = structured polyester, white honeycomb = Reboard.
