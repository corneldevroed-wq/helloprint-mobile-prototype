Product kit icons

Sketch, not linked. The product kits wave from the gap analysis: roller banners, A-boards, table talkers, presentation folders, notepads and booklets, napkins. 28 drawings, not 31: Type on the roller banner uses the same four stands as Colours, the notepad without cover is the bare pad (glued on top), and airlaid and tissue join the material family. Two directions per kit, at 100 px and in the real option rows (40 mobile, 50 desktop). Green border = the recommended option on live.

Chosen (Cornel, 11-16): A for every kit (A-board: B1 now fits in the box, the frame thinner). My reasons:, flat and front-on like the folds and the size shapes, one dark line, colour only where it is the choice (the stand, the cream napkin):
  • Roller banner A: the whole roll-up reads at once; B is clearer for the stands but becomes a second drawing style next to the size row.
  • A-board A: the poster in its frame is what you buy; B (the A from the side) is clever but abstract.
  • Table talkers A: open tent vs closed triangle from the side is the whole difference; B gets busy at 40.
  • Folders A: the pocket says "folder"; the U from above shows the spine width directly.
  • Notepads A: one sheet with the glued edge in black, like the fold or the corner icons.
  • Napkins A: the turned corner makes it a napkin, and the dashed folds are the folds language.
