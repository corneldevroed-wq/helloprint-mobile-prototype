Brief: the mobile menu on desktop. Today the green category bar shows the 11 level-1 categories as plain links and "All products" does nothing. Live (en-ie) every category opens a hover dropdown with 6 image tiles + a promo card; "All products" is a link to an overview page.

All three use the mobile content 1:1 (the same 6 products per category, "Shop all …", the same images). A · The mobile drawer, as is "All products" opens the same drill-down from the left (366 wide), over a dimmed page. The bar items stay plain links.
  • + Nothing new to build or keep in sync: literally the mobile component.
  • − It repeats the bar that is already on screen; three levels of clicking in a narrow column feels like a phone on a 1512 screen.
  • − Hovering the bar does nothing, while live it does: a step back for returning customers.
B · Mega dropdown, the width of the page My pick Hover a category: its 6 products as square image tiles (the home category card, .cat) in one row, "Shop all …" top right. Hover "All products": the 9 groups as columns of text links, each with its "Shop all".
  • + Uses the width; the products are seen, not read. Same pattern customers know from live.
  • + No promo card (calmer than live); "Shop all" does its job.
  • − One new component (the panel); the tiles and links are existing parts.
C · The mobile panel as a small dropdown Hover a category: the mobile level-2 list (thumb, name, "Shop all …") drops down under that item, 320 wide. "All products" becomes a link to an overview page (as live).
  • + The mobile list 1:1, so mobile and desktop look like one menu.
  • − Tall and narrow (about 550 high for 7 rows), most of the screen stays unused; small thumbs.
  • − Needs an All products page that does not exist yet.
