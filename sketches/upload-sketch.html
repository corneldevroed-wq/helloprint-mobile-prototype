What moves where: everything in A and C (the questions, the file check, fit / fill, page counts) doesn’t disappear, it moves to after the order: prepress fixes the small things (bleed, RGB, turning) and emails about the big ones (wrong size, missing pages, blurry). The only thing left in the popup is a failed upload, because then we have nothing to check.

The risk: a problem we find later can push the delivery date. Worth agreeing with the team how fast prepress replies, and saying it in the email (“reply before 16:00 and your date stays”).

Why A: it asks nothing and assumes nothing. The customer drops whatever they have (one PDF, two JPGs, a 16-page book) in one place, and sees the list we print from. Open questions for the team

  1. Several designs: one cart line per design (my pick, simple and clear prices), or one line with a quantity split (like the sizes mix on flyers)? What does production prefer?
