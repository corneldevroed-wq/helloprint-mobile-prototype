Design guidelines

We recommend using our templates and guides for preparing your print-ready PDF. We also accept JPEG, PNG and EPS.

  • Use CMYK as the colour modeNot RGB. Use FOGRA39 (ISO Coated v2). We can convert it for you, but colours may differ from the original.
  • Resolution of at least 300 dpiFor all images and graphics in your artwork.
  • Add 3 mm bleed, keep 4 mm safety marginKeep important elements 4 mm from the edge. Avoid borders or frames on small formats like business cards.
  • Minimum font size 6 ptEmbed or outline fonts. Small black text should be 100% black (C0 M0 Y0 K100). We don't check spelling.
  • Line thicknessAt least 0.25 pt, negative lines at least 0.5 pt.
  • No overprint, max. 300% ink coverageUnless overprint is an effect you want.
