Brief: richer product lines on the desktop cart. Edit / Duplicate / Remove with icons, a quantity upsell, a discount code, the delivery date per line ("Estimated … — latest …") and the product's label as on the product page (badgeHtml: orange = commercial, green = Eco). The page around it is the built cart. Mobile follows once desktop is picked.

Same in all three: label above the name, the discount code under the totals (closed by default: "+ Add discount code" opens the field), and per product line the file deadline and the delivery date (update 11-02, Cornel: "approve your files before" matters per order line, not as one message under the button).

Why per line: every product is made on its own, with its own delivery option, so every line has its own deadline: here the Express flyers need their files approved by Thu 24 Sep, the Saver cards only by Mon 28 Sep. One message under the button can't say that. So the deadline sits right under "Design: …" (who sends the file, and by when), with the delivery date it gives under it: approve by X → estimated Y. Two calm lines with an icon (clock, truck), the time in bold as in today's delivery box; no extra grey block per product. The sidebar loses its delivery box: the dates are on the lines now.

New wording (mine, to check): "Approve your files by 10:30 on Thu 24 Sep", "1000 pcs for €8.51 more · €0.03 each instead of €0.04", "Switch to 1000", "Add discount code". Prices and quantity steps are examples. Icons: Material Symbols Rounded, line, as in the rest of the prototype.

A · Action row under the card, upsell as a light green strip my pick Closest to live: Edit and Duplicate as pills with an icon at the bottom left, Remove on the right (grey, not red: no alarm for a normal action). The quantity upsell is a calm light green strip with one button, so it reads as a tip and not an ad. Most to see, but every part has its own place, so it stays calm.
B · Round icon buttons, quantity as a dropdown Icons only, top right under the price (name in the tooltip). You change the quantity right in the card; the hint next to it does the upsell. The most compact, but icons without words need a hover to be sure.
C · Quiet text links, upsell as one line Edit · Duplicate · Remove as grey text links with an icon, the upsell as one line with a link. The calmest, but the upsell is easy to miss and the actions look secondary (which they are).
