    Business Card Printing in Ireland: Make Every Introduction Count

    A business card is often the first thing people keep from a meeting. Whether you're networking at a conference in Dublin, meeting clients in Limerick or leaving your details at a shop counter, a well-printed card makes sure they remember you. With HelloPrint you design and order business cards online in a few minutes, from everyday classics to cards that truly stand out.

    Start with the card that fits your brand: Classic Business Cards are the best value for money, eco cards are made from recycled, kraft or chlorine-free papers, and PVC cards are durable with the feel of a credit card. Want more impact? Choose deluxe finishes like Spot-UV and coloured foils, special materials, or multilayer cards with a coloured core that shows on every edge.

    Pick a size and shape to match: the standard 85 × 55 mm, rounded corners for a softer look, square cards that stand out in a wallet, the American size or folded cards with room for extra information. Then choose your paper, from silk matte to metallic, pearl marble or wood.

    Not a designer? Choose one of our 1000+ free business card templates or design online, and our team checks your file before it goes to print.
