Starting point: Cornel's artifact: a green hero with "HELP CENTRE" tag, product photos and search, then "Browse by topic" (6 cards, green titles, article-count badges, 3 questions each), then "Still need a hand?" with Chat with Anna / Email us.

What I keep in every concept: search first (it's what people come for), the 6 topics with 3 questions each, and contact at the end. What I take out:

  • The green hero with product photos. On a help page it's decoration that pushes the answers down, and it's a style no other page uses. Instead it gets the category page intro: crumbs, h1, a lead line.
  • The "HELP CENTRE" uppercase tag (the breadcrumb already says it), the green card titles (titles are black) and the "N articles" badges ("View all 12 articles" already says it).
  • The footer "Need help?" line on this page, because the contact block replaces it.

My pick: B. Most people don't come to read; they come to do something: track an order, report a problem, get a quote. The artifact already has those flows (report-a-problem form, quote form), but they're hidden behind topics. B puts them first as our grey tiles, then the topics. A is the safe 1:1, C is the most compact (FAQ style).
